Projection adapters

One replayed state, several consumer shapes.

After replay, Quark has a neutral working state: messages and parts. That state is not sent directly everywhere. Each consumer gets a shape designed for its own boundary.

Records Are the Middle, Not the Source

Projection Rules in Plain Terms

To the LLM

  • user/text can become a simple string content.
  • user/image forces array content with text and image parts.
  • assistant/tool becomes a tool-call part in the assistant message.
  • Completed or errored tools become a separate role: "tool" message.
  • step-start and step-finish are skipped for model context.

To the TUI

  • text becomes a visible message block.
  • image becomes an image attachment in the user message.
  • reasoning becomes a thinking block.
  • tool becomes a tool card, with status and output.
  • step-start and step-finish stay metadata.
Source touchpoints: toModelMessages() in src/session/message.ts and dbToTuiMessages() in src/tui/state.ts.
